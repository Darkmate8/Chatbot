# Hashtag

- A cafe at FLAME University, on the top floor of the Chandragupta Building.
- Overlooks the FLAME Academic Spine. Air-conditioned.
- A common meeting and hangout spot: students come here for games, studying, work, or meetings.
- Plenty of space and seating: chairs, a sofa, and a TV.
- A library of over 300 books, covering all university subjects.
- Menu: vegetarian only.
- Pasta and mains: white pasta, pink pasta, red pasta, lasagna, veg kebab, paneer tikka biryani.
- Salads: Subway veggie salad, paneer salad, green aloo patty salad.
- Snacks: salted fries, masala fries, peri peri fries, nachos (plain, with cheese, with cheese and salsa), onion rings, potato smilies, paneer puff.
- Maggi: cheese, masala, peri peri, double cheese, butter, schezwan, Jain, garlic.
- Garlic bread: plain, cheese, spicy.
- Burgers: aloo patty, Mexican, veg patty, cheese blast, makhani burst, paneer patty, spicy paneer, mushroom, rajma, Italian paneer, whopper.
- Rolls and momos: falafel roll, paneer roll, spring roll, momos, fried momos, paneer momos.
- Desserts: choco muffin, almond muffin, choco cookie, choco cake, brownie with ice cream, hot brownie with ice cream, Biscoff ice cream.
- Coffee: many types, including espressos and cold coffees; flavourings like hazelnut and caramel.
- Other drinks: hot chocolate, tea, milk, fresh lime, virgin mojito, sunrise mojito, soft drinks.
