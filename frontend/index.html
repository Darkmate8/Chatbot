Before we chat

  1. This is an AI chatbot, not a person. It is part of a FLAME University research project about how students feel about campus cafes.
  2. Your messages are saved anonymously for research. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, IP address or device details.
  3. Please do not type your name or anything else that could identify you or someone else.
  4. Your messages are sent to Google's Gemini AI to write the replies. Google does not use them to improve its products.
  5. Because nothing links a chat to you, we cannot find or delete your chat later. All chats are deleted 60 days after they are written.
  6. If a message suggests you might be in crisis, the bot will share helpline numbers, and the research team will see that this happened.
